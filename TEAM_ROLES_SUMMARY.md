# AdventHealth Innovation Platform - Team Roles Summary

## Executive Summary

This document outlines the team structure, roles, and responsibilities required to successfully design, build, deploy, and maintain the AdventHealth Innovation Platform. The project requires a cross-functional team with expertise in healthcare IT, AI/ML, full-stack development, UX design, and change management.

## Project Organization

### Project Governance

**Executive Sponsor**: AdventHealth CIO/CTO
- Final decision authority on scope and budget
- Removes organizational blockers
- Champions platform adoption across 55 hospitals
- Quarterly steering committee reviews

**Product Owner**: AdventHealth Innovation Director
- Defines product vision and roadmap
- Prioritizes features and backlog
- Accepts completed work
- Primary liaison with end users

**Project Manager**: Microsoft or AdventHealth PM
- Day-to-day project coordination
- Risk management and mitigation
- Status reporting and stakeholder communication
- Resource allocation and timeline management

## Communication Plan

### Daily Standups (15 minutes)
- **Attendees**: Core dev team (Tech Lead, AI/ML, Backend, Frontend, QA)
- **Format**: What did you do? What will you do? Any blockers?
- **Time**: 9:00 AM ET

### Weekly Status Meetings (60 minutes)
- **Attendees**: Full project team
- **Agenda**: Progress review, demo, risks, decisions needed
- **Time**: Fridays 2:00 PM ET

### Bi-Weekly Steering Committee (30 minutes)
- **Attendees**: Executive Sponsor, Product Owner, Technical Lead, PM
- **Agenda**: High-level status, budget, major decisions
- **Time**: Every other Wednesday 10:00 AM ET

### Sprint Planning (2 hours, every 2 weeks)
- **Attendees**: Core dev team + Product Owner
- **Agenda**: Review backlog, plan next sprint, estimate work
- **Time**: Monday 9:00 AM ET (start of sprint)

### Sprint Retrospective (1 hour, every 2 weeks)
- **Attendees**: Core dev team
- **Agenda**: What went well? What didn't? Action items
- **Time**: Friday 3:00 PM ET (end of sprint)

## Escalation Path

### Level 1: Team Lead
- **For**: Technical blockers, resource conflicts, minor scope questions
- **Response Time**: Same day
- **Escalate If**: Cannot resolve within 2 days

### Level 2: Project Manager
- **For**: Schedule impacts, budget concerns, cross-team dependencies
- **Response Time**: Within 24 hours
- **Escalate If**: Cannot resolve within 1 week

### Level 3: Product Owner
- **For**: Scope changes, priority conflicts, stakeholder issues
- **Response Time**: Within 48 hours
- **Escalate If**: Requires executive decision

### Level 4: Executive Sponsor
- **For**: Major scope/budget changes, organizational blockers, strategic decisions
- **Response Time**: Within 1 week
- **Escalate If**: Business-critical issue

## Appendix: Job Descriptions

### Technical Lead / Solution Architect

**Full Job Description:**

We are seeking an experienced Technical Lead to architect and guide the development of AdventHealth's Innovation Platform. This role will define the technical vision, make key architecture decisions, and ensure the platform is scalable, secure, and maintainable.

**Responsibilities:**
- Design overall system architecture using Azure cloud services
- Select and validate technology stack (React, FastAPI, Cosmos DB, Azure OpenAI)
- Define integration patterns for Azure OpenAI and Sora video generation
- Establish coding standards and best practices
- Conduct code reviews and provide technical mentorship
- Assess and mitigate technical risks
- Ensure HIPAA compliance and security best practices
- Create and maintain architecture documentation
- Collaborate with Microsoft Azure team on optimization

**Required Qualifications:**
- 7+ years of software architecture experience
- Expert knowledge of Azure cloud services
- Strong experience with Python and modern web frameworks
- Healthcare IT experience (Epic, HL7, FHIR preferred)
- Security and compliance expertise (HIPAA, SOC 2)
- Excellent communication and leadership skills

**Preferred Qualifications:**
- Azure Solutions Architect certification
- Experience with AI/ML integration
- Healthcare innovation platform experience
- Agile/Scrum methodology experience

### AI/ML Engineer

**Full Job Description:**

We are seeking an AI/ML Engineer to design and implement AI-powered agents for the AdventHealth Innovation Platform. This role will integrate Azure OpenAI services, optimize prompts, and ensure high-quality AI-generated insights.

**Responsibilities:**
- Design and implement 5 AI agents (system detection, solution discovery, architecture generation, feasibility scoring, video prompt generation)
- Integrate Azure OpenAI Service (GPT-4o, O3 models)
- Develop prompt engineering strategies for healthcare domain
- Implement Sora video generation integration
- Monitor AI response quality and token usage
- Optimize AI costs while maintaining quality
- Build fallback logic for AI failures
- Create AI monitoring dashboards

**Required Qualifications:**
- 3+ years of AI/ML engineering experience
- Strong Python programming skills
- Experience with OpenAI API or similar LLM services
- Prompt engineering and model fine-tuning experience
- Understanding of healthcare terminology (preferred)
- FastAPI or similar web framework experience

**Preferred Qualifications:**
- Azure OpenAI Service experience
- Healthcare AI applications experience
- Computer vision and video generation knowledge
